Entry for [One Sentence for a Traveler from 1800](https://make.fun/c/u-po-pu-one-sentence-for-a-traveler-from-1800-7e6162fb) on Make.fun — one sentence, at most 200 characters, explaining the modern internet to someone arriving from the year 1800.

## Entry

> Picture every road on earth meeting in one square, where anyone can shout and the whole world hears them at once.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the fixed sentence above to `/output/entry.txt` (113 characters, one sentence, one line, no trailing newline). No network, no randomness, no clock — the output is identical on every run.
